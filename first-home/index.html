Questions

Good to know

Does this affect my credit?

No. There's no credit check here. If you want a full pre-approval later, we'll talk about it first.

What does it cost to work with you?

For most purchases, nothing. Lenders pay brokers when your mortgage funds. If your file ever had a fee, you'd know up front, in writing.

How much down payment do I need?

At least 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the portion above that, up to $1.5M. Less than 20% down means mortgage default insurance, which is added to your mortgage.

Can first-time buyers get a 30-year amortization?

Yes. First-time buyers can now get 30 years on an insured mortgage, which lowers the payment and can raise what you qualify for. This calculator already includes it.
